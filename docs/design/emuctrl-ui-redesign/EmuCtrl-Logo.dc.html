2Refining the E from 1e
The draft E was three equal arms on a stem — a default letterform with no argument behind it. All four are set on one grid and drawn as edge-aligned rectangles, never centred strokes: cap height 60 (ink y18→78), 8px arms, square terminals to match the rail. The middle arm is 4px shorter than the outer arms and its ink sits at y42→50 — centre 46, a 2px lift off true centre (48), which is the standard optical correction; an E with a true-centre middle arm reads bottom-heavy. Every mark is measured against that grid, so what differs between them is only what the arms are. Note this board sits off the bound design systems for the same reason the app does — the cyber-tactical dark direction comes from your brief.
